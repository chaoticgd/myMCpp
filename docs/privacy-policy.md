# Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy for the myMCpp mobile apps by SternXD.

## Data

myMCpp doesn't collect any data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, and it doesn't use the internet.

## Memory card files

It opens the memory card files you pick and edits them when you tell it to.

## Deleting your data

If you uninstall it, anything it saved goes with it.
